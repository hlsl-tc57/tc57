## 6.3 [SIMT Functions](https://github.com/hlsl-tc57/tc57/pull/220)

The primary discussion regarding this PR was a meta conversation about how the standard should be structured and where terminology will belong. The committee decided to split the conversation and focus on SIMT functions and the chair took an action item to return to a more comprehensive meta conversation at a later time.

The currently-open PR uses “may” and not “must” due to the fact that not all implementations are able to fulfil the requirement entirely as written, either due to legacy reasons or hardware constraints. [See comment for further detail](https://github.com/hlsl-tc57/tc57/pull/220#discussion_r4390731154).

All discussions on the pull request were resolved and the committee reached consensus to merge.

## 7.2 Holiday meeting schedule

The 24 November meeting of TC57 will take place on the week of the US Thanksgiving holiday, which is frequently taken as vacation by US workers. There appeared to be enough interest in the 24 November meeting that it will continue as expected.

The meeting scheduled for 22 December is two days before Christmas Eve, which is often a holiday week in many places around the world. The chair suggested that meeting to be cancelled and there were no objections.

The 05 January meeting will remain on the calendar, though the committee may revisit on 08 December after more workers have solidified their winter holiday plans.
