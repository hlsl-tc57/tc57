# 6 Discussions

## 6.2 groupshared resources ([hlsl-tc57/tc57#170](https://github.com/hlsl-tc57/tc57/issues/170))

The committee discussed the issue, and reached consensus that it identified a defect, and should go through the defect process.

## 6.3 Const correctness ([hlsl-tc57/tc57#137](https://github.com/hlsl-tc57/tc57/issues/137))

**Gregory Roth** (Nvidia) raised a concern regarding potential breaking changes in the associated proposal, regardless of how the compiler treated it in the past. **Justin Bogner** (Microsoft) requested a small code example to help understand how the change would be a breaking one, which Gregory offered to pass along.

**Chris Bieneman** (Microsoft) wrote a simplistic example of the behaviour under discussion in [hlsl-tc57/tc57#137](https://github.com/hlsl-tc57/tc57/issues/137), to confirm all parties were discussing the same scenario.

Ultimately, Gregory Roth explicitly refrains from blocking, and Nvidia will continue to discuss internally.

## 6.4 Overload resolution

Implementations may or may not be able to conform with overload resolution changes at the time of publication, but the committee agrees that the changes are worth the pain in the long run.

The decisions made in the overload resolution proposal will impact any other proposal impacting the standard library.

## 6.5 Math Conversion Functions ([hlsl-tc57/tc57#179](https://github.com/hlsl-tc57/tc57/pull/179))

The math conversion functions PR, being one of those changes which is impacted by overload resolution, will seek approval to merge at the next meeting. Delegates are requested to review quickly so concerns can be resolved before that time.
